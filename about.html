About KenduriSafe

Protection for the host,certainty for the caterer.

KenduriSafe is a catering booking platform built for kenduri. Every booking comes with a written agreement, staged payments held by a regulated payment gateway, and a caterer whose identity has actually been checked.

Like Shopee Guarantee, but built for kenduri. Money is only released when each part of the job is done.

Where your money waits

Paid in stages, released when the job is done.

An example RM10,000 booking. Before the event, the caterer only receives what their ingredient receipts show. The rest stays locked until the food arrives.

Curlec is the safe. We’re the remote control.

KenduriSafe never holds your money. Payments sit with Razorpay Curlec, a regulated Malaysian payment gateway. We only tell it when each stage can be released.

  1. At booking

    Pay a 50% deposit

    RM5,000, paid within 3 days of the caterer accepting. It’s held, not handed over. Booking less than 14 days before the event? You pay 100% upfront.

  2. Any time after

    Ingredient money, against receipts

    The caterer uploads ingredient invoices or receipts. You can see every one, and only that exact amount is released so they can start buying.

  3. 14 days before

    Headcount locks, 100% secured

    Final guest count is set (extra guests mean a top-up) and the remaining 50% is paid. If it isn’t, the booking cancels automatically.

  4. Event day

    Tap “Makanan Diterima”

    The caterer uploads timestamped delivery photos. You confirm the food arrived and everything except the final 20% is released.

  5. 48 hours after

    Final 20%, automatically

    No dispute raised? The last 20% releases on its own. Nobody can hold the caterer’s money hostage.

How we verify caterers

Five checks before a caterer can take a single booking.

eKYC with a live face check

IC scan plus a live selfie (turn left, turn right) matched to the IC photo. Stops stolen ICs.

SSM or LHDN registration

A registered business, or LHDN registration for home-based caterers.

Bank name match

The payout account must be in the same name as the IC or SSM record.

Semak Mule check

Our team checks the bank account and phone number against PDRM’s Semak Mule.

Background scan

AI searches the web for scam reports. A person on our team makes the final call.

Being honest

No check can stop someone from choosing to cheat. What verification does is make them fully traceable, with a complete evidence pack ready for the police.

If something goes wrong

A clear process, with a deadline at every step.

Raise a dispute within 48 hours of the event. If a verified caterer runs off with ingredient money, our protection fund refunds the host.

  1. Freeze

    Any money still held stays locked. Nothing moves until it’s settled.

  2. Evidence

    Both sides submit evidence within 3 days. The side that doesn’t submit loses.

  3. Mediation

    Our team reviews the agreement, chat, photos and receipts and decides within 7 days.

  4. Escalation

    Still unresolved? It goes to the Consumer Claims Tribunal or a police report, with the full evidence trail.
