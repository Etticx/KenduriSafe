What you get

Everything a small caterer needs to say yes with confidence.

100% secured before you cook

The full amount is paid in 14 days before the event. If it isn’t, the booking cancels and you don’t cook.

Ingredient money upfront

Upload your market receipts any time after the deposit. That exact amount is released to you, so you never front the cost.

Headcount that stays put

Guest numbers lock 14 days before. Extra guests after that are a paid top-up, not your loss.

A written agreement

Menu, pax, price, delivery time and cancellation policy, agreed by both sides before any money moves.

Paid automatically

Once delivery is confirmed, or 48 hours pass with no dispute, the money releases on its own. No chasing.

A verified badge

Pass our checks once and hosts can see you’re real. Reviews only come from real bookings.

When you get paid

Your money, on a schedule you can plan around.

Payments are held by Razorpay Curlec, a regulated Malaysian payment gateway, and released to your bank at each milestone. Payouts usually land in about two working days.

    Ingredient money

How to join

Get verified once, then take bookings with a badge.

The same checks that protect hosts are what make your business stand out from the ones that disappear.

  1. Create your profile

    Add your menus, price per pax, the areas you cover and how many guests you can handle.

  2. Verify who you are

    Scan your IC and take a live selfie, turning left and right, so we can match it to your IC photo.

  3. Verify your business

    Add your SSM registration, or LHDN registration if you cook from home, and a bank account in the same name.

  4. We run our checks

    Our team checks your bank account and phone number on PDRM’s Semak Mule and looks for scam reports online.

  5. Start taking bookings

    Your verified badge goes live and hosts can book you, with every payment protected.
